Step reveal

One idea at a time

Add class="step" to anything and it waits for the next key press.

  • Good for walking through an argument in the order the author builds it.
  • Good for a question first, answer second.
  • Going back with ← hides steps again, one by one.

Reference

Shortcuts

  • → Space next step or slide
  • ← back
  • Home / End first / last slide
  • Swipe left or right on a touch screen
  • O all slides
  • N my notes
  • T light / dark
  • F full screen · Ctrl+P prints to PDF
